SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE HIGHLIGHTS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Reverse-Auction C2B Market Feed: Tailored to Accra (East Legon, Madina, Makola,
  Kaneshie, Osu, Spintex) and Kumasi (Kejetia, Adum, KNUST).
• Distributed 2-Phase Commit (2PC) Saga Escrow Engine:
  - Phase 1 (Prepare / Lock): Shopper deposits funds via MTN MoMo / Telecel Cash / AT Money into platform vault.
  - Phase 2 (Commit / Release): Shopper inspects delivered groceries and triggers payout to merchant.
  - Compensating Transaction (Abort / Refund): Automatic 100% rollback upon arbitrated dispute.
• Machine Learning Price Benchmark Engine:
  - 1.18x Accra/Kumasi Supermarket Retail markup regression baseline with perishable volatility weights.
• Cryptographic Chain of Custody:
  - Immutable SHA-256 state hashes signed on every 2PC transition with verifiable digital receipts.
